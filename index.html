2026 demand, 1998 infrastructure.

If Caracas is the administrative capital, Valencia is Venezuela's real economy: where goods are made and distributed.

  • Next to the country's main port. Puerto Cabello is 50 km away by road.
  • Venezuela's largest industrial zone. Consumer goods, pharmaceuticals and manufacturing in continuous operation, with a steady flow of executives and engineers.
  • Supply that has fallen behind. Hotels charge around US$110 a night for outdated rooms, and short-term rentals are in the hands of private owners with no common standards.
  • Three asset types. Executive suites in the Zona Norte (Guaparo, El Viñedo, La Trigaleña and Prebo), warehouses in the industrial zones and land with development potential.

We control the asset from start to finish.

We don't build from scratch. We buy what already exists, transform it and manage it through to its sale.

  1. Identify

    We look for buildings, warehouses and land with untapped value in Valencia and its industrial zones, often before they reach the market.

  2. Acquire

    We buy after rigorous due diligence, and only at an entry price that leaves a margin from day one.

  3. Transform

    We convert each space into a property that is self-sufficient in power and water.

  4. Manage

    We run every suite with US dollar contracts, maintenance and a 24-hour concierge.

  5. Sell

    Once the asset is stabilized, we sell it to institutional buyers, funds or REITs.
